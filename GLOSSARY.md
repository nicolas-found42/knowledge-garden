# Knowledge Garden

A personal collection whose sources become automatically maintained knowledge for reading, searching, and navigation. These terms follow [Specification #1](https://github.com/nicolas-found42/knowledge-garden/issues/1).

## Language

**Collection**:
The sources, pages, facts, relationships, and saved views managed together by the owner.

**Source**:
Material supplied for ingestion, such as a file or an explicitly supplied URL. A link found inside a source is a reference to its destination, not evidence that the destination's contents have been acquired.

**Source version**:
A particular state of a source, ordered using reliable version or date evidence when available. Receiving the same content again does not make it a new version.

**Page**:
A readable, navigable unit of knowledge about a source, entity, event, or topic. A page can contain granular facts and relationships as well as source-grounded prose.

**Fact**:
A specific piece of knowledge about an entity or event, with its value, qualifications, and support. The wiki presents one current value for the same fact; different events can have different values.

**Relationship**:
A typed connection between knowledge items, with direction where meaningful, qualifications, and supporting evidence. Similarity does not by itself establish identity, causation, or another stronger connection.

**Evidence**:
Acquired source content that supports a fact or relationship, together with its origin and location. A source's qualifications and uncertainty remain part of that support.

**Transcript segment**:
A passage recognized from a recording and associated with an approximate interval in the original audio. It is machine-transcribed evidence whose wording and speaker attribution remain unverified.

**Event**:
A distinct occurrence described by a source, including an observation or reasoning event. Its date is separate from the date on which the collection received or processed the source.

**Manual correction**:
Legacy authored material that was previously allowed to override a fact. It must be preserved during migration as recoverable source evidence; future external edits do not have authority over generated knowledge.

**Saved view**:
A legacy named search, set of filters, relationship scope, or navigation arrangement. Existing views remain readable; users do not create or edit persistent views.

**Trail**:
An ordered route through existing material. The owner may arrange a temporary trail for navigation; new persistent trails are not authored or reordered.
