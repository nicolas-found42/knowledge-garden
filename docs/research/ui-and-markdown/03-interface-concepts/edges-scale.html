Synthetic graphs: lines, points and grouped summaries

Automatic measurement of 1,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 nodes with 2 or 8 directed edges per node. One warmup and three measured repeats per configuration. Every measured run renders the full graph and a 12-group summary. These are authored deterministic fixtures, not semantic clustering or a force-layout benchmark.

Full renders submit all edges, including 400,000 lines at 50k × 8, in bounded batches of 5,000. Group summaries aggregate directed endpoint pairs; at most 144 weighted group links and 12 nodes are drawn. Arrowheads are omitted in both renderers: edge direction is retained in data and group counts, not visually distinguished.
