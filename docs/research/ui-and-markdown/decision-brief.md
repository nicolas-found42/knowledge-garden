## Validation gates the spec should retain

- **Actual ordinary Obsidian:** open a fresh temporary vault and test links/backlinks/properties/embeds, external edit refresh, internal rename, external move and unsupported-original opening. It was not installed here. No custom plugin may be needed, and the app may not drop capabilities for compatibility.
- **External authoring integrity:** atomic saves, concurrent app/editor changes, invalid frontmatter, duplicate IDs, conflict recovery, app restart and missing original revisions. The sequential hash test is necessary evidence, not a concurrency proof.
- **Source/answer fidelity:** naturally selected tasks over actual mixed files, labels fixed independently, separate missed-source/unsupported-answer/incorrect-anchor metrics. Retain failures, qualifiers and acquisition state.
- **Usability and accessibility:** counterbalanced same-task comparison; successful source recovery, extra items inspected, time, keyboard focus/back navigation, reader confidence and clutter preference. Agent critique/browser scripts are not participants.
- **Scale and app delivery:** representative graph density/media/labels, stable expand/collapse, accessible navigation, cold rebuild/indexing and signed Mac bundle. Current Canvas timings do not substitute for these.

Prior suggestions for an answer-change inbox, method rehearsal, counterfactual workbench and analogy lab remain proposals from the earlier wave. They are not newly confirmed requirements or hidden additions to this specification.
