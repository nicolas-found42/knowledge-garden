## Research boundaries

- Read-only access to original personal files, sessions and setups. Relevant selected excerpts may be evaluated through the configured API. Raw personal material, private URLs and exact source mappings stay in local research storage outside this repository.
- URLs discovered in conversations are occurrence records only. Their destinations are not fetched. Independently selected public research sources and datasets may be acquired.
- Acquisition state (`captured`, `merely linked`, `could not retrieve`) is distinct from epistemic status (`recorded`, `tentative`, `speculative`). A captured model observation remains derived material.
- Preserve historical records and failed attempts. Freeze evaluation labels before calls; distinguish authored development examples, holdout examples and actual-source observations.
- Separate candidate-generation, conversion, semantic and workflow failures. Report provider usage and supporting-model costs separately; do not infer total system cost from Jev alone.

The experiments and synthesis are recorded in the concern reports. Storage technology, default navigation, automatic promotion, proactive interruption, tool retention and message granularity remain open.
