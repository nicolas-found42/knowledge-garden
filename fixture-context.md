# Ticket 13 fixture context

The frozen independent labels are `src-tauri/tests/fixtures/photos/expected-labels.json` at commit `3c91d04`, SHA-256 `7734ba8afee7299b4b20aa3f28f166e0cb832e6464bdbaa8bc318efaeb1129bf`. The labels were committed before image construction or any model call.

Deterministic fixtures are generated by the external `create-photo-fixtures.swift` script. It uses AppKit/CoreGraphics drawing and ImageIO to emit a 480x360 JPEG with EXIF `DateTimeOriginal` plus conflicting IPTC caption, a PNG with machine-readable sign text and no photo metadata, a simple HEIC with a plant/pot scene, and an invalid JPEG payload. `fixture-manifest.json` records their digests.

The pictures are flat synthetic shapes, not natural photographs. They can test metadata, OCR, container decoding and provenance paths; model correctness on ordinary natural photos still needs broader independently labeled evaluation. The malformed JPEG starts with a JPEG marker but ImageIO cannot decode a full image.
